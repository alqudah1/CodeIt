## Required interpretation

These are historical product-activity records. They are not verified paying
customers, revenue, or proof of product-market fit. The database may include
internal, demonstration, or test accounts.

The historical system did not store login events or a login counter. Login
frequency must not be claimed or estimated from these records. The last-active
field is incomplete and must not be described as complete retention data.

## Privacy rule for demonstrations

Only aggregate totals and distributions should be shown to investors,
influencers, or other outside parties. Do not expose usernames, names, email
addresses, birthdays, parent emails, password hashes, or individual student
activity.

Use the private Admin Evidence page for demonstrations. The Users and individual
student pages are operational administration tools, not presentation material.
