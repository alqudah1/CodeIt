## What to measure

Check `/admin/funnel` once per day, not every hour.

1. Challenge views
2. Challenge starts
3. Projects generated
4. Projects saved
5. New accounts
6. Returning learners

Do not redesign the page from fewer than 20 attributable visits unless there is a clear functional failure. After 20–50 visits, improve the largest drop:

- Low view → start: the promise or game choices are unclear.
- Good starts, low generation: builder speed or reliability is the problem.
- Good generation, low saves: the save/account value is unclear.
- Good saves, low returns: the next mission and competition loop need work.
